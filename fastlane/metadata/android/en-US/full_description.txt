Conatus for Android is the phone app for Conatus, the self-hosted task manager. Your tasks live on the phone and sync with your server in the background, so the app works offline and sends your changes once it has a connection. It also works without a server, as a task list that stays on the phone.

• Three layouts. Comfortable has big rows and tabs at the bottom, for one hand. Compact fits a task on each line. Web looks and works like the web app.
• Today, Upcoming with a month calendar, projects with sections and sub-tasks, labels, search, Completed and Trash
• Quick add that reads dates, repeats, priorities, projects and labels from the task name, offline too
• Reminders as notifications, scheduled on the phone, so they arrive without a connection
• Recurring tasks, deadlines, durations, priorities and comments
• A long press selects tasks, to complete, move, reschedule or delete several at once
• A Today widget, a Quick Settings tile, launcher shortcuts and new tasks from text shared by other apps
• Sign-in with a password or through the browser, which covers single sign-on
• Light and dark themes, large text and TalkBack

To sync, you need a Conatus server running 1.3.2 or newer. It needs HTTPS unless it's on your own network.

This build has no Google code. It syncs when it opens, after each change and every hour.
